  - [x] **Atlas-scale runner hardening** (2026-06-11): three pre-full-atlas fixes from a code review of the runner. (1) **Incremental partition writes + per-group failure tolerance** — `run_pipeline` now dispatches via `client.submit` + `as_completed` and writes each `part-<id>.parquet` the moment its tile group finishes; a failed group is logged and recorded in the manifest (`counts.failed_groups`, `aggregate_stats.task_errors`) instead of aborting. Previously a single `dask.compute` barrier wrote nothing until *all* tasks succeeded, so one `KilledWorker` at hour 3 of the atlas run would have discarded every completed partition with nothing for `--resume` to pick up. (2) **Task-graph slimming** — each task now embeds only its group's `fetch_tile_ids` URIs instead of the full 13,373-entry `tile_uri_map` (~MBs per task → multi-GB graph at ~1000–2000 groups); additionally, when pre-warm is skipped (Coiled), one paginated S3 LIST of `lidar_prefix` drops tiles absent from the bucket before dispatch (~75% of indexed tiles have no LAZ), replacing thousands of worker-side 404 round-trips and dead tasks. (3) **Process-stable RNG seeds** — `_seed_for_polygon` was using the builtin `hash()`, which is salted per process (`PYTHONHASHSEED`), so every worker drew different RANSAC/bootstrap seeds and runs were not reproducible despite the PRD §10 claim; now sha256-derived. 2 new unit tests (seed pinned to an exact value; corrupt-tile group fails without aborting the run + `--resume` retries it), 145/145 pass; distributed path verified end-to-end on a 2-worker LocalCluster with a deliberately corrupt tile (partial success + manifest errors + resume-retry all confirmed).
  - [x] **Extended mounting taxonomy** (2026-06-12): three new rule-based labels on top of PRD §7.5's six. (1) **R1 flat/pitched split** — `flush_mount_rooftop` is replaced by `flush_mount_flat_roof` / `flush_mount_pitched_roof`, keyed on `roof_tilt_deg` vs a new `mounting_rules.flat_roof_tilt_deg_max` (default 7.0°; flat commercial roofs drain ≤ ~5°, lowest common pitch 2:12 ≈ 9.5°). The split is label-only — R1's predicate and confidence are unchanged. (2) **R7 `east_west_rack_rooftop`** — promotes the M5 `east_west_rack` multi-plane flag to a mounting type; evaluated *before* R1/R2 (otherwise R2 absorbs the primary plane as a generic tilted rack). Gable-facet guard: two facets of a pitched roof also face ~180° apart, so when a roof fit exists, confidence is graded on the roof being flat (`flat_roof_tilt_deg_max`); plus a `tilt_deg_max` 20° cap. (3) **R8 `pole_mount`** — off-building + HAG ≥ 2 m + area ≤ 20 m² + aspect ≤ 2; evaluated before R3 so carport keeps only the elongated arrays (small near-square elevated systems previously landed in `ambiguous`). Plumbing: `MountingFeatures` gains `roof_tilt_deg` + `east_west_rack` (defaults keep the ABC backward-compatible); `MOUNTING_LABELS` is now 9 labels; `configs/default.yaml`, README enumeration, and `scripts/_review_bench.py` colours updated. 8 new/updated rule tests (suite at 152); thresholds are uncalibrated defaults — revisit once a labeled validation sample exists (see "Open improvements" below). Note for cross-run comparisons: pre-0.2 outputs say `flush_mount_rooftop`, and the config hash differs from the May benchmark runs because `MountingRulesConfig` grew.
  - [x] **Classification robustness: rooftop vs canopy vs ground** (2026-07-27): six changes from a review focused on the three-way superclass split, all aimed at converting "confidently wrong" into either "correct" or "ambiguous + flagged". (1) **NaN height-above-ground propagates** — `tile_task.py` coerced NaN HAG to 0.0 before classification and output, so a canopy with no nearby ground reference fired R4/R5 as a *confident ground mount*; NaN now flows through (the rules already treated NaN as non-evidence), `height_above_ground_m` is nullable in `OUTPUT_SCHEMA`, and the same fix applies to `is_tracker_suspected` inputs. (2) **Canopy evidence from under-panel returns** — new `ground_under_polygon` (`geometry/heights.py`) counts ground-class returns *inside* the polygon and their vertical gap to the panel plane: rooftops have ~none (the building blocks the pulse), open-sided canopies have many. Wired through `MountingFeatures` (`n_ground_under`, `ground_under_gap_m`); in `rules.py` it (a) reroutes footprint-mapped carports away from R1/R2, (b) substitutes for missing HAG in R3/R8 elevation evidence, (c) suppresses R4/R5 on contradiction. This makes the superclass split primarily LiDAR-driven rather than footprint-driven. Knobs: `mounting_rules.canopy_min_ground_points_under` (15), `canopy_gap_m_min` (2.0 m). (3) **`on_building` overlap gate** — a sliver footprint intersection no longer counts; requires `roof_plane.min_overlap_frac` (0.5) of the polygon's area. (4) **R3/R8 height caps + `possible_missing_footprint` flag** — carport capped at 6 m, pole mount at 8 m (soft, via the confidence margin; caps don't veto when HAG is unknown); off-building > 6 m HAG without canopy evidence gets the new quality flag and typically lands `ambiguous` (previously: confident carport for rooftops on unmapped buildings). (5) **Nearest-k HAG fallback** — when the 10 m centroid disk has no class-2 return (large commercial roofs), use the 50 nearest ground points within 100 m (`heights.ground_fallback_k` / `ground_fallback_max_radius_m`); fixes the systematic `ambiguous` sink for big flat-roof arrays. (6) **Class-1 fallback cutoff 1.5 → 0.8 m** (`io.classification.fallback_height_above_ground_m`) — 1.5 m deleted the lower half of ground-mount arrays in tiles without class 6, biasing tilt/HAG and starving density. 20 new unit tests (suite at 172), incl. two `_build_row`-level integration tests: a LiDAR-only carport (no footprint involvement) classifies as carport, and a no-ground-reference polygon emits null HAG + `ambiguous`. The runner smoke fixture was made physically consistent (no class-2 returns under the synthetic building — real LiDAR can't see ground through a roof). Config hash changes again (`RoofPlaneConfig`, `HeightsConfig`, `MountingRulesConfig` all grew). Thresholds are principled defaults, still uncalibrated — see Open improvements #3.
  - [x] **`parent_polygon_id` in the output schema** (2026-07-29): settles the MultiPolygon join-back convention ahead of switching to the Mask R-CNN atlas (`pv_prediction_pipeline` S8 `pv_attributes.parquet`, which contains MultiPolygons). ## Open improvements (code review, 2026-06-11/12)

Post-M8 backlog, roughly in recommended order. None block the full atlas run.

1. **Footprint gap fill** — ~8% of polygons get `on_building=False` purely from FEMA gaps (M4 spike finding). *Partially mitigated 2026-07-27*: these can no longer become confident carports (R3/R8 height caps route high-HAG off-building polygons to `ambiguous` + `possible_missing_footprint`, which also makes the affected population directly queryable), but recovering the correct *rooftop* label still needs the footprint. Fall back to Microsoft US Building Footprints where FEMA is empty.
2. **Adaptive erosion** — `panel_plane.erosion_m=0.15` is fixed; on the bottom 10–15% of polygons (~3 m²) it eats a large share of the points and feeds the 42% `low_density` rate seen in the 10k bench. Scale erosion with polygon size.
3. **Labeled validation sample** — ~200 aerial-verified labels (tooling: `scripts/_review_bench.py` + `_aerial.py`) to (a) calibrate the rule thresholds — incl. the uncalibrated 2026-06-12 taxonomy defaults (`flat_roof_tilt_deg_max`, R7 tilt cap, R8 area cap) *and* the 2026-07-27 robustness defaults (`min_overlap_frac`, R3/R8 height caps, `canopy_min_ground_points_under`, `canopy_gap_m_min`) — (b) determine whether the 33% `ambiguous` rate is miscalibration or genuinely bad inputs, and (c) produce the rooftop/canopy/ground superclass confusion matrix + confidence reliability curve the working paper needs before "high confidence" claims go to print.
4. **Installation-level clustering** — post-processing pass grouping polygons (DBSCAN on centroids + tilt/azimuth coherence) into installations: enables utility-vs-commercial-vs-residential site typing, much stronger tracker detection than the per-polygon heuristic (the 1k bench split one plant 617 tracker / 363 fixed), row spacing / GCR, and repairing the SAM3 fragment tail by letting fragments inherit their cluster's plane fit.
5. **Performance** — (a) bbox pre-mask before `contains_xy` in `clip_points_to_polygon` and the roof-ring clip (`roof_plane.py`): every polygon currently scans the full tile-group point pool, most of the ~15-min dense-tile wall time; (b) intra-group polygon chunking (HANDOFF.md Step 4) so the densest tile stops being the longest pole; (c) the per-polygon `other_pvs` GeoDataFrame rebuild in `tile_task.py` is O(n²) in group size — precompute once per group.
6. **Learned mounting classifier (v2)** — gradient boosting on `MountingFeatures` (+ parcel land-use / imagery features) behind the existing `MountingClassifier` ABC; rules stay as fallback/explainer. Explicitly out of scope for v1 per PRD §5.
7. **External validation** — cross-reference a sample against permit/interconnection records (e.g. LBNL Tracking the Sun, AZ coverage) to put accuracy numbers on tilt/azimuth/mounting type for the release.
8. **Carport refinement** — join OSM parking lots / Maricopa assessor parcel land-use to separate parking canopies from shade structures; parcel land-use is also a strong prior for the classifier generally.

## FEMA AZ footprint findings (M4 spike, 2026-05-01)

- ~92% of atlas polygons intersect at least one FEMA AZ footprint; ~8% sit in gaps (FEMA appears to miss some residential structures).
- Among polygons that match, only a fraction are *cleanly* contained — many SAM3 polygons overshoot or sit at the edge of the FEMA footprint, producing tiny intersection fractions.
- FEMA's `shape_area` attribute column is **all zeros** in the dataset; always compute `geometry.area` instead.
- Implication for M2/M6: roughly 1 in 10 polygons will get `on_building=False` (correctly) due to footprint gaps; M5 mounting rules must handle this cleanly. Optional supplement: Microsoft US Building Footprints would close most of the gap if needed.

## Spike (pre-M2)

`scripts/spike.py` — LiDAR data-quality spike, runs as a uv inline script. Two phases:

1. `uv run scripts/spike.py --discover` — lists `s3://asu-nsf-phoenix/data/lidar_data/`, looks for a `tile_index.parquet`, sniffs one LAZ header (CRS, point count, bbox).
2. `uv run scripts/spike.py [--polygon-id ... --tile-uri ...]` — clips class-6 points to one polygon, fits a plane via SVD, writes a 3-panel PNG (plan view, side view, residual histogram) to `scripts/eda_outputs/`.

Default target: detection `21_840480_397711__0` (sam3 score 0.98, ~67 m², east valley). Polygons sourced from `C:\Users\job_t\code\free\pv_sam3\artifacts\atlas\latest.parquet`.

`scripts/_aerial.py` overlays the Maricopa County 2024 ortho (`gis.maricopa.gov/.../Aerial2024Sep2024OctOrtho/MapServer/tile/{lod}/{row}/{col}`, LOD 13 = 7.5 cm/px) under the polygon + LiDAR points so panel boundaries can be visually verified. Disk cache at `~/.cache/pv_geom_aerial/`. Note: ESRI MapServer tiles are built on demand; first request can race the cache build, so `_fetch_tile` retries 4× with linear backoff.

Success criterion: a clear, flat tilted surface in the side view; RMSE ≲ 0.10 m; tilt distribution across ~50 random polygons looks plausible (residential AZ → mode near roof pitch ~20°, flush-mount → near 0°). If RMSE blows up or points are visibly noisy, reconsider the panel-fit threshold defaults in PRD §7.1 before M3.

### Spike results (2026-05-01)

**Phase 1** — single polygon `21_840480_397711__0` (67 m², east valley): tilt=5.9°, az=199°, RMSE=0.022 m. Plane visually clean.
**Phase 2** — 5 more polygons across 5 tiles: 5/6 RMSE < 0.04 m; one outlier at 0.377 m (clear polygon overshoot, RANSAC will handle).
**Phase 3 — batch (`scripts/spike_validate.py batch`, n=29):**
  - density centred on 9–11 pts/m² (PRD assumed 15–18; floor of 4 still safe)
  - tilt distribution bimodal: peak at 5–10° (flush-mount) + secondary at 18–22° (tilted-rack)
  - azimuth concentrated 150–230° (south to SSW), ~zero north-facing — physically correct for AZ
  - 27/29 RMSEs < 0.05 m (PRD threshold)
**Phase 4 — size sweep (`scripts/spike_validate.py size-sweep`, 50 bootstraps × 12 N):**
  - on a clean polygon, tilt is *unbiased* down to N=5 (mean stays at truth ±0.24°)
  - statistical spread falls as 1/√N: tilt std 0.24°→0.01°, azimuth std 1.6°→0.03° from N=5 to N=1000
  - both stay under the 2°/10° threshold at every N tested
  - **Practical floor is RANSAC robustness, not statistical noise**: ~30 inlier points needed to reject overshoot/multi-plane outliers reliably. At ~10 pts/m² density that's ~3 m² minimum — about the bottom 10–15% of polygons (median ~11 m²).
