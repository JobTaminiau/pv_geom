What 0.2.0 does (branch `v0.2-geometry-report`; 254 unit tests, was 194):

- **Vintage is an input.** `--polygon-vintage` (or a per-polygon column) and `--lidar-date`; undeclared, the LiDAR date is measured per tile from GPS time. Every row carries `polygon_vintage`, `lidar_date`, `lidar_date_source`, `vintage_gap_days` and **`geometry_basis`** — `panel_confirmed` (plane ≥ 5 cm above the roof reference), `panel_by_vintage` (polygon no newer than the LiDAR), `surface_unresolved` (coincides with the roof and polygons are newer: flush array or bare roof), `unscreened`, `no_fit`.
- **`pv-geom report <output>`** → `report.html` (self-contained), `report.md`, `methods.md` (paragraph filled with the run's parameters), `summary.json`, `tables/*.csv`, `figures/*.{png,pdf,svg}` at journal column widths, and `dataset/` (consolidated GeoParquet + CSV + data dictionary). Everything is reported per polygon and weighted by array surface, for all fitted rows and per geometry basis; the report leads with the panel-basis rows. Works on pre-0.2.0 outputs (pass the dates).
- **Two inputs suffice.** Footprints are optional (open-ring roof reference; `roof_ref_source` says which). Polygons: any vector format, id column optional. CRS comes from the tile index; non-metric CRSs are refused. `pv-geom inspect-tile` vets a new LiDAR source.
- **Noise-adaptive fit tolerance** (`panel_plane.ransac_threshold_max_m`, flag `wide_tolerance_fit`, column `panel_fit_tolerance_m`) — found on Delaware, see below.
- **Speed/memory**: per-group point grid, per-tile class + near-polygon filtering. Output is valid GeoParquet. Dead config keys removed.

**What Delaware broke (the "leaky abstractions"), all fixed in 0.2.0:**

1. *No id column* in the polygon layer → ids synthesized, `input_row` added as a join key.
2. *Tile-index id column is `NAME`, not `Name`* → auto-detected.
3. *Fit tolerance was Phoenix-tuned.* Delaware returns scatter ~7.5 cm about a roof plane (Phoenix ~2 cm); at the 5 cm tolerance only **24%** of polygons on a test tile reached consensus (80% at 15 cm), and the first run fitted **35%**. 4. *Memory.* DE tiles are 1.5 km at ~20 pts/m² (44M returns, 475 MB LAZ); one tile group was `KilledWorker` on 16 GB. Fixed by keeping only returns near polygons as each tile is read.
5. *Documented S3 paths were stale*: DE LiDAR is at `s3://free-research-data-raw/US/delaware/top-level/lidar/point_cloud/tilecls/`; `…/national/fema_footprints/de.geoparquet` no longer exists (FEMA DE used here: `s3://de-database/data/delaware/pv_tep/inputs-de/footprints.parquet`). `../delaware-pv-geom` still points at the old ones.

7. Like Phoenix, DE tiles have **no class 6**, so the class-1 fallback is the normal path — now above *local* ground rather than one tile-wide median.

**2026-08-03 — VINTAGE SCREEN COVERAGE FIXED (it only reached 25% of rows), + confidence cap, + vintage in the manifest.** Codebase review chasing the vintage question found the screen added on 07-31 was structurally limited: `no_panel_standoff` needs a roof reference, and on the v0.1.0 atlas **only 87,499 of 349,233 rows (25.1%) had one**. The cause was a one-line default, not anything intrinsic: `extract_roof_plane` never passed `min_inlier_frac` to `fit_plane_ransac`, so the *ring* fit inherited the panel fit's 0.6 consensus floor. A ring buffered around an array straddles facets and eaves, so 60% single-plane consensus routinely fails — **163,716 rows (46.9%) were rejected as `roof_complex` with a median ring RMSE of 5.8 cm, and 163,715 of them would have passed the 10 cm `rmse_max_m` gate.** Downstream, those rows fell to R2's no-roof fallback, which labelled **90,894 rows (26% of the atlas) `tilted_rack_rooftop` at median confidence 1.00 on a median panel tilt of 19.3°** — residential roof-pitch range, i.e. bare pitched roofs read as racks.

Changes: (1) `roof_plane.min_inlier_frac` (0.4) plus a **collar guard** — the naive loosening is *unsafe*, measured on real polygons from the cached Phoenix tile: 3 of 7 newly-recovered rows fitted the facet **across the ridge**, 20–45° off, which corrupts `panel_roof_angle_deg`/`height_above_roof_m` worse than no fit at all. The guard refits on the collar (ring points within `collar_m` of the array) when the wide-ring plane doesn't explain it: wrong-facet cases 3/7 → **0/8**, max disagreement 44.9° → 10.2°. (2) `roof_complex` split into `roof_no_consensus` (no plane agreed) vs `roof_complex` (agreed but RMSE over the gate); neither is usable, and `RoofPlaneResult.usable` is now the single place that decides. (3) New `standoff_unscreenable` flag — the screen is tri-state (failed / not-applicable / passed), so "no flag" finally means "screened and passed". (4) `mounting_rules.no_panel_standoff_confidence_max` (0.5) caps confidence on flagged rows: they scored *maximally* on R1 (**34,873 rows at ≥0.999**), so filtering on high confidence selected precisely the unmeasured rows. (5) `vintage.input_epoch` config + `_probe_lidar_vintage`: the run measures its own LiDAR epoch from **per-point GPS time** and warns on a gap. GPS time matters — the Phoenix tiles were **flown 2020-11-26/28** but the LAS header says 2021-06-30 (delivery), a 7-month overstatement; `configs/phoenix.yaml` now declares the 2024 imagery epoch. (6) Manifest gains a `vintage` block and `aggregate_stats.standoff_screen` (counts + cross-tab vs `mounting_type` + `height_above_roof_m` percentiles).

Measured end-to-end on the 37 real polygons of the one cached tile: usable roof references **4 → 11**, unscreenable 33 → 26, rows at confidence ≥0.999 20 → 16, and **5 of 37 rows moved R2 → R1** (`tilted_rack_rooftop` → `flush_mount_pitched_roof`) — exactly the predicted failure mode. Suite 177 → **194**; ruff/mypy at their prior baselines. ⚠ **Config hash changes** (`RoofPlaneConfig`, `MountingRulesConfig`, new `VintageConfig`). **A rerun is needed for this one** — unlike the 07-31 flag, these change `mounting_type`, `mounting_confidence`, and which rows carry roof geometry, so v0.1.0 partitions cannot be corrected post-hoc. 1. **Footprint gap fill** — ~8% of polygons get `on_building=False` purely from FEMA gaps (M4 spike finding). *Partially mitigated 2026-07-27*: these can no longer become confident carports (R3/R8 height caps route high-HAG off-building polygons to `ambiguous` + `possible_missing_footprint`, which also makes the affected population directly queryable), but recovering the correct *rooftop* label still needs the footprint. 2. **Adaptive erosion** — `panel_plane.erosion_m=0.15` is fixed; on the bottom 10–15% of polygons (~3 m²) it eats a large share of the points and feeds the 42% `low_density` rate seen in the 10k bench. Scale erosion with polygon size.
3. **Labeled validation sample** — ~200 aerial-verified labels (tooling: `scripts/_review_bench.py` + `_aerial.py`) to (a) calibrate the rule thresholds — incl. the uncalibrated 2026-06-12 taxonomy defaults (`flat_roof_tilt_deg_max`, R7 tilt cap, R8 area cap) *and* the 2026-07-27 robustness defaults (`min_overlap_frac`, R3/R8 height caps, `canopy_min_ground_points_under`, `canopy_gap_m_min`) — (b) determine whether the 33% `ambiguous` rate is miscalibration or genuinely bad inputs, and (c) produce the rooftop/canopy/ground superclass confusion matrix + confidence reliability curve the working paper needs before "high confidence" claims go to print.
4. **Installation-level clustering** — post-processing pass grouping polygons (DBSCAN on centroids + tilt/azimuth coherence) into installations: enables utility-vs-commercial-vs-residential site typing, much stronger tracker detection than the per-polygon heuristic (the 1k bench split one plant 617 tracker / 363 fixed), row spacing / GCR, and repairing the SAM3 fragment tail by letting fragments inherit their cluster's plane fit.
5. **Performance** — (a) bbox pre-mask before `contains_xy` in `clip_points_to_polygon` and the roof-ring clip (`roof_plane.py`): every polygon currently scans the full tile-group point pool, most of the ~15-min dense-tile wall time; (b) intra-group polygon chunking (HANDOFF.md Step 4) so the densest tile stops being the longest pole; (c) the per-polygon `other_pvs` GeoDataFrame rebuild in `tile_task.py` is O(n²) in group size — precompute once per group.
6. **Learned mounting classifier (v2)** — gradient boosting on `MountingFeatures` (+ parcel land-use / imagery features) behind the existing `MountingClassifier` ABC; rules stay as fallback/explainer. Explicitly out of scope for v1 per PRD §5.
7. **External validation** — cross-reference a sample against permit/interconnection records (e.g. LBNL Tracking the Sun, AZ coverage) to put accuracy numbers on tilt/azimuth/mounting type for the release.
8. **Carport refinement** — join OSM parking lots / Maricopa assessor parcel land-use to separate parking canopies from shade structures; parcel land-use is also a strong prior for the classifier generally. *Superseded 2026-07-30 by the parking-lot-first redesign (pick-up item #1): the validation showed location layers, not LiDAR evidence, are the carport discriminator.*
9. **Roof-plane facet selection** — ***likely fixed 2026-08-03; needs verification after the rerun.*** Found 2026-07-30 via pv-cooling's facet analysis: on gable roofs the roof ring sometimes fits the facet *opposite* the panel — 4,834 output rows have panel azimuth ~180° from `roof_azimuth_deg` with `panel_roof_angle_deg` ≈ the sum of both pitches. The **collar guard** added 2026-08-03 (`roof_plane.collar_m` / `collar_agreement_min`, `_enforce_collar_agreement`) targets exactly this: when the wide-ring plane doesn't explain the ring points adjacent to the array, the fit is redone on that collar. ### Spike results (2026-05-01)

**Phase 1** — single polygon `21_840480_397711__0` (67 m², east valley): tilt=5.9°, az=199°, RMSE=0.022 m. Plane visually clean.

  - tilt distribution bimodal: peak at 5–10° (flush-mount) + secondary at 18–22° (tilted-rack)
  - azimuth concentrated 150–230° (south to SSW), ~zero north-facing — physically correct for AZ
  - 27/29 RMSEs < 0.05 m (PRD threshold)
**Phase 4 — size sweep (`scripts/spike_validate.py size-sweep`, 50 bootstraps × 12 N):**
  - on a clean polygon, tilt is *unbiased* down to N=5 (mean stays at truth ±0.24°)
  - statistical spread falls as 1/√N: tilt std 0.24°→0.01°, azimuth std 1.6°→0.03° from N=5 to N=1000
  - both stay under the 2°/10° threshold at every N tested
  - **Practical floor is RANSAC robustness, not statistical noise**: ~30 inlier points needed to reject overshoot/multi-plane outliers reliably. At ~10 pts/m² density that's ~3 m² minimum — about the bottom 10–15% of polygons (median ~11 m²).
